No Phase A0–A4 kill criterion has been formally evaluated yet, and none is
claimed to have fired. Phase 1 is pre-A1 groundwork: it builds the baselines
that criterion A1 is measured against.

Two Phase 1 results bear directly on later criteria and should be carried
forward:

* **A0 is partially answered and the answer is worse than assumed.**
  Point-in-time forecast weather for GB begins **2024-02-04**, not 2021 (row 4).
  `docs/research_plan.md` plans around "~5 winters"; the real figure is ~2.5.
  This does not fire the A0 kill criterion — a usable archive does exist — but
  it materially reduces the power of every downstream test, including A2's
  Diebold-Mariano.
* **A3 should be treated as live rather than remote.** NESO's forecast error
  has no detectable relationship to heating demand on the crudest test
  available (r = −0.013 against forecast HDD over 921 days; no degradation in
  the coldest decile). This is preliminary and underpowered, and HDD is a far
  cruder proxy than the bottom-up model A1 would build — it is **not** a
  verdict. But it is the obvious first place to look, and it came back empty.
  See `reports/phase1_findings.md`.

Both findings are on the **electricity** target. `docs/research_plan.md`
recommends gas LDZ offtakes as the primary target, and neither finding
transfers to the gas side unexamined — two of the three components of the
electricity gap (embedded renewables, transmission metering) have no gas
analogue.
